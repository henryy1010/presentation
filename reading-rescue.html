How to play

  1. Form two teams. Give each team a name and choose one spokesperson.
  2. Take equal turns. Each team gets six challenges: two in every round. There is no stealing.
  3. Reveal and discuss. The host reveals each challenge and starts the optional timer: 45, 60, or 75 seconds.
  4. Lock your answer. Correct answers earn 100, 150, or 200 points. An incorrect answer or timeout earns zero. Ordering challenges require the complete correct sequence.
  5. Learn from the feedback. Read the explanation together, then continue. The highest score wins; equal scores mean a draw.
